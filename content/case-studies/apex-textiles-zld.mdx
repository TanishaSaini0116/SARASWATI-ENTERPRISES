# The Challenge
The client was facing strict regulatory action due to high TDS and color in their effluent. They needed a complete ZLD system to recover water and avoid factory closure.

# The Solution
We installed a 500 KLD ETP followed by a 3-stage RO system and a Multi-Effect Evaporator (MEE).

# The Results
- 95% water recovery for reuse in dyeing
- Complete compliance with SPCB norms
- ROI achieved in 24 months through salt and water recovery
